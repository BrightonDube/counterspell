## Idea

Measure how many opposite positions on a rune strip disagree when two wardens read it from its ends.

## Why it is original

The Mirror Gate setting, signal rules, wording and examples were written for Counterspell. The task asks for a count of disagreements across the full strip.

## Patterns used

Scan a string from both ends and count pairs that meet a condition. Stop the scan where the positions meet or cross.

## Checklist answers

The task uses a single string parameter and returns an integer. The statement and schema use the same alphabet and length bounds. The worked example shows what happens to matching pairs, mismatched pairs and an unpaired rune.

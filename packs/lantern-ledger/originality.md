## Idea

An archway lantern in the Grimoire vault has an integer charge meter. Count the entries in its ledger that leave the meter at the inspection level.

## Why it is original

The vault inspection scenario, rule for recording a mark after each entry, wording and examples were developed for this pack.

## Patterns used

A single ordered scan maintains a running total and counts exact matches.

The function uses supported integer types. The statement follows the schema, works the first sample and explains repeated matches, empty ledgers and the opening reading. The private cases were hand-derived, including the maximum length and both incorrect programs. The four hints advance from a question to an update rule without using case values. Metadata gives time and extra space.

# The Diagonal Total

## Task
Given a grid of whole numbers, add up the cells on its main diagonal: the cells whose row number and column number are the same.

## Input
A grid of whole numbers, as a list of rows. Every row has the same length.

## Output
One whole number: the total of the diagonal cells. A grid with no rows or no columns has a total of 0.

## Examples
The public examples are listed in the pack's samples.

## Constraints
The grid has at most 6 rows and at most 6 columns. Each cell is from -9 to 9.

# Catalogue Batch 02 Requirements

## Theme

Two pointers and sequence scans: opposing scans, partitioning, deduplication and pair or triplet relationships.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs: four easy, four medium and two hard.
- Provide complete Python, JavaScript, Java and C++ starters, references, hints and visible or hidden tests.
- Include boundary cases for empty, singleton, repeated and already-ordered inputs.
- Keep every statement and test corpus original.
- Publish only after originality, review, sandbox and approval gates pass for all 10 packs.

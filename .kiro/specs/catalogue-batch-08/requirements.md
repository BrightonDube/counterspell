# Catalogue Batch 08 Requirements

## Theme

Tries, strings and prefix systems: dictionaries, autocomplete, segmentation and prefix aggregation.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs: four easy, four medium and two hard.
- Include complete Python, JavaScript, Java and C++ assets and Unicode or character-set constraints.
- Provide original hints, references, tests, generators and ghosts.
- Test empty strings, shared prefixes, duplicate words and absent paths.
- Require complete-batch review, sandbox validation and approval before publication.

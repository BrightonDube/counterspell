# Catalogue Batch 03 Requirements

## Theme

Stacks, queues and expression processing: nesting, monotonic structures, simulation and bounded buffering.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs with a four easy, four medium and two hard split.
- Support Python, JavaScript, Java and C++.
- Include malformed-expression, empty-structure and duplicate-value cases where relevant.
- Supply complete original statements, four hints, references, tests and known ghosts.
- Do not publish any pack in the batch if any review, sandbox or approval gate fails.

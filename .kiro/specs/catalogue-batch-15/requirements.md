# Catalogue Batch 15 Requirements

## Theme

Greedy methods and intervals: exchange arguments, scheduling, merging and coverage.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs with four easy, four medium and two hard.
- Provide complete Python, JavaScript, Java and C++ starters, references and hints.
- Include touching, nested, duplicate and zero-length interval cases where relevant.
- Make the greedy invariant explicit enough to review for correctness.
- Publish only after originality, static, review, sandbox and approval gates pass.

# Catalogue Batch 01 Design

Cover counting, duplicate detection, grouped signatures, prefix-frequency queries, sparse indexing and multi-key joins. Vary input shapes across numbers, strings, records and small matrices.

At least three packs must expose a tempting quadratic ghost, two must exercise overflow or large-count handling, and one hard pack must combine prefix information with a hash-indexed invariant.

Use the standard public/private pack split and the publication gate from `completion-program`.

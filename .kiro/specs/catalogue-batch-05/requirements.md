# Catalogue Batch 05 Requirements

## Theme

Binary search and ordered decision spaces: exact lookup, boundaries, rotated order and answer-space search.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs with a four easy, four medium and two hard distribution.
- Include complete assets for Python, JavaScript, Java and C++.
- Cover absent answers, duplicate boundaries, integer extremes and minimal feasible values.
- Provide original statements, hints, references, tests, generators and known ghosts.
- Require full-batch review, sandbox and approval before publication.

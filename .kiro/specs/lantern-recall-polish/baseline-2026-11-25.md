# Lantern recall — baseline (2026-11-25, pre-LR01)

Captured from `web/src/routes/break/memory/+page.svelte` + `web/src/lib/break/memory.js`.

## Gameplay
- 3x3 grid (`TILES = 9`), sequence starts at 3 entries and grows by one per round
  (`newRound` appends `floor(rng()*9)`); `level(seq) = seq.length - 2`.
- Playback (`showSequence`): each entry lights `lit` for 600 ms, then 200 ms dark.
  Reduced-motion skips lighting and announces the whole sequence textually, 300 ms.
- Press: verdict via `checkPress`; `flashTile`+`flashKind` colour flash for 400 ms.
  Correct-continue announces "Correct.", round_done announces "Correct. Next round."
  and re-shows after 800 ms; wrong ends the round and finalises the score.
- Score: `session.load()` first; signed-in POSTs `{ game: 'brain', level }`,
  signed-out shows the sign-in nudge, probe failure shows a save error.

## Accessibility
- Roving `tabindex` over `focusIdx`; arrows move by ±1/±3, Enter/Space presses.
- `aria-live="polite"` region announces every lit tile, verdict and phase change.
- Tiles are `<button aria-label="Tile N">` — no imagery today.

## Performance
- Page ships zero media: no `/art/` fetches, no preloads. The only network after
  load is the optional `/api/auth/session` probe and the scores POST.
- Styling is ~15 lines of scoped CSS + utility classes; transition is
  `transition-colors` only.

# Catalogue Batch 11 Requirements

## Theme

Graph traversal and connectivity: components, reachability, grids, bipartite structure and dependency order.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs with four easy, four medium and two hard.
- Support Python, JavaScript, Java and C++ with complete parsers and references.
- Cover disconnected, cyclic, duplicate-edge and isolated-node cases.
- Include original hints, tests, generators and known ghosts.
- Publish only after full-batch review, sandbox validation and approval.

# Catalogue Batch 12 Requirements

## Theme

Weighted and advanced graphs: shortest paths, spanning structures, union-find and state-expanded routing.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs: four easy, four medium and two hard.
- Provide complete Python, JavaScript, Java and C++ starters, references and bounded graph parsers.
- Cover unreachable nodes, equal weights, parallel edges and large costs.
- Include original statements, hints, tests, generators and ghosts.
- Do not publish the batch if any review, sandbox or approval gate fails.

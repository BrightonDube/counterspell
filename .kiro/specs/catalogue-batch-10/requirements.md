# Catalogue Batch 10 Requirements

## Theme

Backtracking and constraint search: combinations, placements, partitions and bounded state exploration.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs: four easy, four medium and two hard.
- Include complete Python, JavaScript, Java and C++ starters, references and pruning guidance.
- Keep constraints bounded so correct references pass sandbox limits.
- Supply original hints, tests, generators and known ghosts.
- Hold publication until all packs pass originality, review, sandbox and approval.

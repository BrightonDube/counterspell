# Catalogue Batch 07 Requirements

## Theme

Trees and binary search trees: traversal, aggregation, reconstruction, ancestry and ordering invariants.

## Requirements

- Author 10 original packs with four easy, four medium and two hard.
- Support Python, JavaScript, Java and C++.
- Cover empty, skewed, duplicate-valued and deep trees within safe limits.
- Include original hints, references, visible and hidden tests and known ghosts.
- Do not publish the batch until every pack passes review, sandbox validation and approval.

# Stage S0: topic and concept selection

## Inputs
- The backlog entry for the slug in `docs/pack-backlog.md`.
- `packs/catalogue.toml` (tracks and concepts).
- The names of existing folders under `packs/` and the private tree (names only, no contents).

## Do
1. Confirm the slug is not used (`SLUG-01`).
2. Confirm the track and every concept appear in `packs/catalogue.toml` (`TAX-01`).
3. Confirm the difficulty is `easy`, `medium` or `hard`.
4. Confirm a backlog entry exists for the slug (`BRF-01`).
5. Write `.kiro/pipeline/state/<slug>.toml` with `stage = "S0"` and `status = "in_progress"`.

Keep your working brief in session notes. Do not put it in the state file.

## Output
- `.kiro/pipeline/state/<slug>.toml`, with only the allowed keys: `slug`, `stage`, `status`.

## Stop condition
All four checks pass. Otherwise stop, and report the code only.

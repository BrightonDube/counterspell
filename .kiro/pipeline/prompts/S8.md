# Stage S8: difficulty and complexity note

## Inputs
- The S4 reference and the S2 statement.

## Do
In `packs/<slug>/meta.toml` (public), set:

- `difficulty`: `easy`, `medium` or `hard`, matched to the concept count and the length of the reference.
- `complexity_note`: 240 characters or fewer. It states the time and the extra space of the reference, and gives one short reason.

Do not add `reference_complexity` or a `[bench]` table in this stage. The efficiency work adds them later (efficiency-familiar T40).

## Output
- `packs/<slug>/meta.toml` (public), with the two fields.

## Stop condition
`GRM-18` passes and the complexity note is 240 characters or fewer.

## Checklist items this stage must answer
- `RV-difficulty-1`: the concept count and the reference length match `difficulty`.

## Rules
- Use measured or stated facts only. Do not claim a complexity you did not derive from the reference.

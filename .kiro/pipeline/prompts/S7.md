# Stage S7: hints

## Inputs
- The S2 statement and the S4 reference.

## Do
Write `hints.toml` (public) with exactly four rungs:

- Rung 1, a nudge: point at the idea without naming a step.
- Rung 2, an approach: name the general technique.
- Rung 3, pseudo-code: a short outline of at most three lines in a fenced block.
- Rung 4, a near-solution step: one step short of the full answer.

Each rung is general. It must not assume the learner's specific bug. No rung may contain a sample expected value, a hidden expected value or a known-ghost `got` value (`HNT-03`). No fenced block may be longer than three lines (`GRM-27`).

## Output
- `hints.toml` (public).

## Stop condition
`GRM-26`, `GRM-27` and `HNT-03` pass. The leak check reads the private values in memory and reports only the rung number.

## Checklist items this stage must answer
- `RV-hints-1`: rung 1 is a nudge only.
- `RV-hints-2`: rung 3 is pseudo-code of three lines at most.
- `RV-hints-3`: no rung names a bug the learner may not have.

## Rules
- Do not quote a value from the private tree in a hint, even to explain it.

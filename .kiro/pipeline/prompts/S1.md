# Stage S1: originality record

## Inputs
- The S0 brief for the slug.
- Your own wording. Use general techniques only.

## Do
Write `packs/<slug>/originality.md` in the public tree with these level-two sections, each with at least one line of text:

1. `## Idea`: the idea in one paragraph, in plain words.
2. `## Why it is original`: why this scenario and wording are your own.
3. `## Patterns used`: general techniques, such as a single pass with a lookup table. Patterns only, never wording.
4. `## Checklist answers`: answer each item in `.kiro/pipeline/checklists/author.md` for this stage.
5. `## Third-party text avoided`: state that no third-party statement was opened while drafting.

## Output
- `packs/<slug>/originality.md` (public).

## Stop condition
The record has all five sections with text (`ORG-02` passes), and no marker from the originality list appears in any public or private file (`ORG-01` passes).

## Rules
- Do not open or quote any third-party problem statement while drafting (PIP-3).
- Do not copy a title, a scenario, a sample or a trade-dress element from anywhere.
- Do not put any hidden value in this record.
